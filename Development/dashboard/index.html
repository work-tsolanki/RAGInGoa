How this works
01You speak or type in any of 14 Indian languages. Sarvam transcribes live; the query is embedded as-is.
02Dense vectors (ChromaDB) and keyword hits (bm25s) are merged over a live corpus of 858,768 passages.
03Groq (Claude as fallback) generates the answer, streamed live. Every answer is checked back against the retrieved passages — off-topic or ungrounded questions are declined or hedged, not guessed.
