How this works
01You speak or type in any of 14 Indian languages. Sarvam transcribes live; the query is embedded as-is.
02Dense vectors (ChromaDB) and keyword hits (bm25s) are merged over a live corpus of 858,768 passages.
03Groq (Claude as fallback) generates the answer, streamed live. Every answer is checked back against the retrieved passages. Off-topic or ungrounded questions are declined or hedged, not guessed.

Notice
Think of this as a general-knowledge lookup, not a search engine. It can't answer about specific people, current events, or local details. Broad questions like "What is a corporation?" or "How does photosynthesis work?" tend to get a confident answer. Very specific, local, or "right now" questions usually come back uncertain or with no answer, that means it's being honest about not knowing, not that it's broken.
